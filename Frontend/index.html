How to play
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of its base.
  • Move all four tokens around the board and into the center.
  • Land on a rival token to send it back to its base. Star squares are safe.
  • Rolling a 6, capturing, or reaching the center gives you another roll.
  • Three 6s in a row ends your turn.
  • You need the exact number to reach the center.
